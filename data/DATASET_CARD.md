# Original technical fixtures
Six French concept labels support controlled lookup only. All trajectories were originally authored mathematically for this repository and are dedicated under CC0-1.0. No captured gestures, people, corpus clips or third party motion assets were used.

**Synthetic technical fixtures — NOT VALIDATED LSF.** These movements do not claim to express their associated concepts. Linguistic and animation reviews remain pending. Cleared rights concern these original fixture files only. The directory name `approved_motion_assets` denotes local technical preview eligibility, never linguistic approval or publication approval.

Coordinates are meters: X right, Y up, Z forward. Time is milliseconds; 10 FPS, inclusive endpoints 0–1000 ms. Nine positional joints; no finger configuration, facial grammar, rotations or physical skeleton constraints. Linear interpolation is intended. No evidence of intelligibility exists. Replace fixtures with authorized, reviewed movements before any accessible LSF publication.
