# SignaCulture implementation status — 2026-11-17

**Overall goal remains incomplete.** A local experimental ingestion/lookup/3D preview/audio/export studio works. No general text-to-gloss translator, linguistically validated signing avatar or state-of-the-art result has been established. This document supersedes historical implementation snapshots; it does not erase earlier experiments.

Current evidence is collected in [`research/CURRENT_RESEARCH_STATUS.json`](CURRENT_RESEARCH_STATUS.json), regenerated by `python3 scripts/audit_research_status.py`. That script checks saved artifacts/hashes and reports measured failures; it cannot perform linguistic review.

## Essential next input

A qualified France-LSF or Belgian LSFB reviewer must verify annotation/translation alignment and representation completeness, or a newly available appropriately licensed, genuine parallel corpus must supply that evidence. The France-LSF [review packet](text-to-gloss/fsl/FSL_TRAINING_REVIEW_PACKET.md) contains 61 pending pairs with source annotation evidence. No authorized local video copy was verified for those recordings. The separate Belgian review pack at `research/private/lsfb-alignment-audit/video-review/index.html` contains video and synchronized hand-tier annotations. Review is pending, not silently assigned to the software.

Before additional large training, freeze reviewed data and an adequately supported conversation/recording holdout. More epochs, a larger model or more subtitle-only rows cannot be claimed as a remedy for missing supervision. Calibrated 3D reconstruction and fluent-signer comprehension assessment remain separate outstanding requirements.
